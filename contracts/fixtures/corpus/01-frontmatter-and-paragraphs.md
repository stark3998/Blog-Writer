This is the lede. It is a single line, as every paragraph in the dialect is.

A second paragraph follows after exactly one blank line. Sentences can be long; the dialect never hard-wraps prose, so diffs stay readable in review tools that soft-wrap lines.

Plain punctuation needs no escaping: commas, periods, colons: semicolons; question marks? exclamation marks! Parentheses (like these), quotes "double" and 'single', hyphens - en dashes – em dashes — and ellipses...
