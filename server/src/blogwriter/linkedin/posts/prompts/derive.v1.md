Task: write one LinkedIn post derived from the writer's approved article in `<material>`.

- Pick one angle from the article that stands on its own in a feed: one decision and its
  trade-off, one finding, one checklist, one failure and its fix. Do not summarize the whole
  article, and never mention the article, the blog or "my latest post": the reader gets the
  value here, without clicking anything.
- Lead with the writer's own experience when the material has it (`<story>`, `<pov>`,
  `<interview_quote>`). Research supports a point; it does not lead the post.
- `<other_posts>`, when present, holds the first lines of posts already derived from this
  article. Choose a different angle and a different hook type, and, when `<task>` lets you
  choose the format, a different format.
- `<brief_angles>` lists angles and hook seeds from the research. Use one if it fits the
  material; they are suggestions, not facts.
- `angle`: one sentence naming the angle you chose, for the writer's review.
