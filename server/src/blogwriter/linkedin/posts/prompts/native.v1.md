Task: write one native LinkedIn post from the writer's story and position bank in `<material>`.
There is no article and no research behind it: the writer's own material is the whole post.

- Build the post around one story or one position. When there are several, pick the one with
  the most specific detail and use the others only if they sharpen it.
- Open inside the situation: what the writer was looking at, what broke, what had to be decided.
  Never open with a general statement about the industry.
- Keep the decision and its trade-off visible: what the writer chose, what they gave up, and how
  they knew it worked (or did not).
- Keep anonymized details anonymized. Do not add a client name, an industry, a headcount, a
  location, a date or a number that the material does not state.
- End on what the writer would tell a peer to do or check, in one or two plain sentences. No
  question to the reader.
- `angle`: one sentence naming the story or position the post is built on.
