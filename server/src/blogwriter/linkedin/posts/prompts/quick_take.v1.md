Task: write a quick take on a piece of news. `<idea>` says what happened, `<research>` items
hold what the sources found, and `<owner_take>` or `<pov>` is the writer's position in their own
words.

- The writer's take leads. Within the first three sentences say what the writer thinks and why,
  reusing their own wording where it is strong.
- Then the one or two facts from the research the take rests on, each with its publisher and
  title in the sentence (see the system rules on attribution). Nothing else from the news.
- Then what a practitioner should do or check this week, in plain sentences.
- When the sources disagree or the news is unconfirmed, say so plainly instead of picking a side.
- Text format only. Keep within the length in `<task>`.
- `angle`: one sentence naming the take.
