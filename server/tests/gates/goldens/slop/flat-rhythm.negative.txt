I moved 2,000 apps. It took 14 months, three rollbacks and one very long weekend in March when the SCIM connector silently dropped every user whose department code had a trailing space. Nobody noticed for a day. The fix was one line. Finding it took a week of diffing exports, reading connector logs and asking HR why their feed changed format in 2019 without telling anyone. Lesson learned. Now every wave starts with a dry run against a copy of the HR feed, and the dry run has to come back clean before anyone touches production. Boring works.
