Last March I moved 2,000 apps from Okta to Microsoft Entra ID over 14 months. SCIM provisioning broke for 37 of them because the attribute mapping used extensionAttribute3, which our HR feed never populated. We fixed it with a two-week freeze and a script that diffed the Okta profile against the Entra user object. The script found 412 mismatches, and most were stale department codes from a 2019 reorg.
