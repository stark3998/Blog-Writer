# Voice & Style Guide (version 4)

## Openings

Starts with the incident and the month ("Last March we moved").

## Never

No questions to the reader.

## Rules

Prefer:
- Give the number with its unit, then what it changed.

Avoid:
- Do not open with a question to the reader.

Never use these phrases:
- let's dive in
- game-changer

Vocabulary:
- identity provider: spelled out
- MFA
- token: per tool call

## Exemplars

Passages the writer wrote. Match how they read: sentence length, openings, word choice, how numbers and decisions are stated. Never reuse their content or facts.

The last 600 took the rest.
