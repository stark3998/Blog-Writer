Plain *emphasis*, **strong**, and ***both at once***.

Intraword emphasis works with asterisks: un*frigging*believable and **bold**face.

Nested: **bold with *italic* inside** and *italic with **bold** inside*.

Punctuation inside: *"quoted"*, **(parenthesised)**, and *trailing period.*

This is (*really*) important. **Note:** labels end with a colon.

Code inside emphasis: **the `kubectl` command** and *`inline` first*.

Links inside emphasis: **see [the docs](https://example.com/docs)** and emphasis inside links: [**bold link text**](https://example.com/bold).

Adjacent spans with a space: *one* *two* and **three** **four**.

Emphasis across a whole sentence: *Every word here is emphasised, including the full stop.*

Spaces around a lone asterisk keep it literal: a \* b and 5 \*\* 2.
